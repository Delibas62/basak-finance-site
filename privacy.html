Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy covers the Basak Finance website and the "Basak Finance Publisher" application (the "App"), operated by the owner of the Basak Finance channel ("we").

What the App does

The App publishes videos produced for the Basak Finance channel to the channel's own social media accounts. It is operated solely by the channel owner, on the channel owner's accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data we access

When the channel owner authorizes the App with a platform (for example TikTok via Login Kit and the Content Posting API), the platform issues access tokens for that account. With those tokens the App reads basic profile information of the authorized account (display name, avatar, open id) and uploads videos to it. The App does not access, collect or store information about any other person, follower, viewer or account.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored encrypted-at-rest on a private server controlled by us, are never shared with third parties and are used only to perform the uploads described above. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the platform's account settings, after which the App can no longer act on the account.

Cookies and analytics

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent or share any data. Videos are uploaded to the platforms the owner chooses (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok); those platforms process the uploaded content under their own policies.

Children

The website and App are not directed at children under 18 and knowingly collect no data from them.
