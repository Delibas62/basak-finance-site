Basak Finance Publisher

Private publishing console for the Basak Finance channel. It connects the channel's own TikTok account through Login Kit and uploads finished episodes with the Content Posting API.

1. Connect TikTok account

Scopes requested: user.info.basic (show which account is connected), video.upload (send an episode to the inbox as a draft), video.publish (post an episode directly to the profile).

Connect TikTok

Not connected.

2. Publish an episode

After authorization the publisher service picks the next finished episode, uploads the 1080×1920 mp4 and either places it in the creator's inbox (draft) or posts it directly with the chosen privacy level. Progress is reported back here and to the owner's Telegram.

Episodes are our own editorial videos: narrated finance history, flat-poster illustrations, no third-party material.
